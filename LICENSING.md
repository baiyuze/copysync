### 1. GNU AGPL-3.0, free of charge

The full text is in [LICENSE](LICENSE). Anyone can use, study, modify and share CopySync under it, including companies. In return, if you:

- distribute CopySync or a modified version (as an app, inside another product, or preinstalled on devices), or
- let people use a modified version over a network (for example, a modified signaling server offered as a service),

you must make the complete source code of your version available to those users under the AGPL-3.0, including your changes and the code you combine with it.

### 2. Commercial license, paid

For organizations that can't or don't want to meet those terms. Typical cases:

- shipping CopySync, or code from it, inside a closed-source product;
- modifying CopySync for internal or customer use without publishing the changes;
- running a modified server as a hosted service without releasing its source;
- needing terms the AGPL doesn't offer, such as a warranty, support or indemnity.

To ask about a commercial license, [open a "Commercial license" issue](https://github.com/baiyuze/copysync/issues/new?template=commercial-license.yml). Issues are public, so leave out anything confidential; we'll continue in private.

### Earlier versions

Versions 1.2.0 and earlier were released under the MIT License, and those releases stay under it.

### Contributions

By submitting a contribution, you license it to everyone under the AGPL-3.0 and grant baiyuze the right to also distribute it under commercial licenses. This is what keeps the commercial license possible.

### Third-party components

Libraries that CopySync uses keep their own licenses (MIT, BSD, Apache-2.0 and similar), all compatible with the AGPL-3.0.
